# Interactive Meeting Preparation Runbook

A systematic, guided approach to meeting preparation that ensures every meeting has clear outcomes, effective participation, and productive follow-up integration with your GTD system.

## Purpose

This runbook helps you:
- **Transform meeting preparation** from reactive to strategic
- **Define clear meeting outcomes** and success criteria
- **Prepare effective agendas** and talking points
- **Optimize your participation** and contribution strategy
- **Plan follow-up actions** and accountability systems
- **Integrate meeting outcomes** into your GTD workflow
- **Build meeting leadership skills** regardless of your role

## Prerequisites

1. **Meeting details** (time, participants, general purpose)
2. **GTD system** for capturing actions and follow-ups
3. **Calendar access** for scheduling preparation and follow-up time
4. **10-20 minutes** of focused preparation time

## Instructions for AI Agent

This is a practical and strategic runbook. Ask **ONE question at a time**, help users think through meeting dynamics and outcomes, and create actionable preparation plans. Focus on making meetings more productive and less draining.

### Phase 1: Meeting Analysis & Context

**Step 1: Meeting Assessment**

```
🤝 **Welcome to Interactive Meeting Preparation!**

Let's transform your upcoming meeting into a productive, outcome-focused session.

**Question 1:** Tell me about the meeting you're preparing for:

**Basic Details:**
• **Meeting Topic/Title:** What's the official purpose?
• **Duration:** How long is it scheduled?
• **Participants:** Who will be there? (names, roles, stakeholders)
• **Your Role:** Are you leading, participating, presenting, or observing?
• **Format:** In-person, video call, phone, or hybrid?

**Initial Assessment:**
• How do you feel about this meeting? (excited, neutral, dreading)
• What's your biggest concern or hope for the outcome?

[Gather basic meeting context and initial feelings]
```

**Step 2: Meeting Type & Strategy**

```
**Question 2:** What type of meeting is this, and what's the best approach?

**Meeting Type Classification:**
A) 🎯 **Decision-Making** - Need to reach conclusions and commitments
B) 💡 **Brainstorming/Creative** - Generating ideas and solutions
C) 📊 **Information Sharing** - Updates, reports, status reviews
D) 🤝 **Relationship Building** - Team building, networking, connection
E) 🔍 **Problem Solving** - Analyzing issues and developing solutions
F) 📋 **Planning/Coordination** - Project planning, resource allocation
G) 🎓 **Learning/Training** - Education, skill development, knowledge transfer

**Meeting Dynamics:**
• **Power Structure:** Who has decision-making authority?
• **Personality Mix:** Any strong personalities or quiet voices to consider?
• **History:** How have similar meetings with this group gone?
• **Stakes:** How important is this meeting to your goals/projects?

Understanding the type and dynamics helps us plan your optimal approach.

[Classify meeting type and assess group dynamics]
```

### Phase 2: Outcome Definition & Success Criteria

**Step 3: Desired Outcomes Clarification**

```
**Question 3:** What would make this meeting a success from your perspective?

**Outcome Categories:**
🎯 **Decisions:** What decisions need to be made?
📋 **Actions:** What actions should be committed to?
📝 **Information:** What information do you need to gather or share?
🤝 **Relationships:** Any relationship building or alignment needed?
🧠 **Understanding:** What should everyone understand by the end?

**Your Success Criteria:**
Complete these sentences:
• "This meeting will be successful if we..."
• "I personally need to accomplish..."
• "The group should leave with..."
• "The biggest waste would be if we..."

**Realistic Expectations:**
Given the time, participants, and format:
• Are your desired outcomes achievable?
• What's the minimum viable outcome?
• What would be a bonus or stretch outcome?

[Define clear, realistic success criteria]
```

**Step 4: Stakeholder Needs Analysis**

```
**Question 4:** Let's consider what other participants need from this meeting.

**Key Participants Analysis:**
For each key person attending:

**[Participant 1 - Name/Role]:**
• What are they hoping to get from this meeting?
• What information do they need to share or receive?
• What decisions do they need to make or influence?
• Any concerns or resistance they might have?
• How can you help them be successful?

**[Continue for key participants]**

**Group Dynamics:**
• Any conflicting agendas or priorities?
• Who are the natural allies for your outcomes?
• Anyone who might need extra consideration or preparation?
• What shared interests can unite the group?

**Value Creation:**
How can you make this meeting valuable for everyone, not just yourself?

[Understand and plan for stakeholder needs]
```

### Phase 3: Agenda Development & Structure

**Step 5: Agenda Creation Strategy**

```
**Question 5:** Let's design the meeting structure and agenda.

**Your Influence Level:**
• Are you creating/controlling the agenda?
• Can you suggest agenda items or structure?
• Do you need to work within someone else's agenda?
• Any opportunity to influence the format or flow?

**Agenda Design Principles:**
🎯 **Outcomes-First:** Start with desired outcomes, then plan activities
⏰ **Time-Bounded:** Specific time allocations for each section
🏗️ **Logical Flow:** Build from context to decisions to actions
👥 **Engagement:** Include everyone meaningfully
📝 **Capture:** Built-in documentation and action planning

**Proposed Meeting Structure:**
1. **Opening** (5 min) - Context, objectives, agenda review
2. **Information Sharing** ([X] min) - Updates, reports, context setting
3. **Discussion/Analysis** ([X] min) - Core work, problem-solving, ideation
4. **Decision Making** ([X] min) - Conclusions, commitments, agreements
5. **Action Planning** (10 min) - Next steps, owners, timelines
6. **Closing** (5 min) - Summary, follow-up, next meeting

Does this structure work for your meeting type and goals?

[Design optimal meeting structure and agenda]
```

**Step 6: Key Discussion Points**

```
**Question 6:** What are the critical discussion points and questions for this meeting?

**Information You'll Share:**
• What updates or information should you provide?
• Any data, reports, or insights to present?
• Stories or examples that would be valuable?
• Questions you should ask to gather information?

**Key Discussion Topics:**
For each major agenda item:

**Topic:** [Discussion point]
• **Your Position:** What's your viewpoint or recommendation?
• **Supporting Evidence:** Data, examples, reasoning to share?
• **Questions to Ask:** What do you need to learn from others?
• **Potential Objections:** What resistance might you encounter?
• **Bridging Language:** How can you find common ground?

**Strategic Questions:**
What questions could you ask to:
• Uncover hidden concerns or motivations?
• Guide the group toward good decisions?
• Ensure all voices are heard?
• Keep discussions productive and focused?

[Prepare key talking points and strategic questions]
```

```
• **Connections:** How can you bridge different participants or ideas?

• **Challenges:** How will you handle difficult participants or moments?
• **Facilitation:** Any opportunities to help guide discussion?
• **Support:** How can you help others be successful?

**Energy Management:**
• What will help you stay engaged and focused?
• Any strategies for handling stress or conflict?
• How will you maintain a constructive attitude?

[Plan optimal participation approach]
```


### Phase 6: Follow-Up Planning

**Step 9: Post-Meeting Action Strategy**

```
**Question 9:** Let's plan how you'll capture and follow up on meeting outcomes.

**Capture Strategy:**
📝 **Note-Taking Approach:** How will you capture key points during the meeting?
🎯 **Action Item Tracking:** System for recording commitments and next steps?
📊 **Decision Documentation:** How will you note key decisions and agreements?
👥 **Follow-Up Contacts:** List of people you might need to connect with after?

**Immediate Post-Meeting (first 30 minutes):**
• **Brain Dump:** Capture all thoughts, insights, and actions while fresh
• **Action Processing:** Convert meeting outcomes into GTD tasks and projects
• **Calendar Updates:** Schedule any follow-up meetings or deadlines
• **Communication:** Any immediate follow-up messages needed?

**GTD Integration:**
How will meeting outcomes integrate into your GTD system?
• **New Projects:** Any projects that should be created from meeting outcomes?
• **Next Actions:** Actions you committed to, with proper contexts?
• **Waiting For:** Items you're waiting for others to complete?
• **Someday/Maybe:** Ideas or possibilities to capture for later?

**Follow-Up Communications:**
📧 **Meeting Summary:** Will you send recap notes to participants?
🤝 **Individual Follow-Ups:** One-on-one conversations needed with specific people?
📅 **Scheduling:** Next meetings, check-ins, or deadlines to coordinate?
📋 **Deliverables:** Any commitments you made that need project planning?

[Plan comprehensive follow-up approach]
```

```
**Question 11:** Let's prepare for potential challenges and how you'll handle them.

**Common Meeting Challenges:**
🕰️ **Time Problems:** What if the meeting runs long or short?
💬 **Participation Issues:** What if someone dominates or others don't contribute?
🔥 **Conflict/Tension:** How will you handle disagreements or personality clashes?
😐 **Low Energy:** What if the group seems disengaged or unmotivated?
📍 **Off-Track Discussion:** How will you help redirect unproductive tangents?
❓ **Unclear Outcomes:** What if the meeting ends without clear decisions/actions?

**Your Specific Concerns:**
Based on this group and meeting:
• What's most likely to go wrong?
• Which participants might be challenging?
• What topics might create conflict or confusion?
• Any technical or logistical risks?

**Response Strategies:**
For your top 2-3 concerns:
**[Concern 1]:** How will you respond if this happens?
**[Concern 2]:** What's your strategy for handling this?
**[Concern 3]:** How can you help redirect if this occurs?

**Backup Plans:**
• Alternative ways to achieve your objectives?
• Graceful ways to suggest process changes?
• Exit strategies if the meeting becomes unproductive?

[Prepare for likely challenges and develop response strategies]
```
