# Interactive Time Audit Runbook

A systematic, guided analysis of your time usage patterns that reveals the gap between intention and reality, identifies optimization opportunities, and creates actionable plans for better time alignment with your goals and values.

## Purpose

This runbook helps you:
- **Analyze actual time usage** versus perceived and intended time allocation
- **Identify productivity patterns** and time management strengths/weaknesses
- **Discover time drains** and hidden time wasters
- **Optimize time allocation** to align with goals and values
- **Create sustainable systems** for ongoing time awareness and improvement
- **Build time estimation skills** for better planning and commitments
- **Design ideal time structure** that supports your priorities and energy

## Prerequisites

1. **Time tracking data** (at least 3-7 days of recent activity)
2. **Calendar access** to review scheduled vs. actual time use
3. **GTD system** with current goals and priorities
4. **Honest mindset** for objective analysis and improvement

## Instructions for AI Agent

This is an analytical and reflective runbook. Ask **ONE question at a time**, help users see patterns without judgment, and focus on actionable improvements. Use data to drive insights while remaining encouraging and solution-focused.
