# Interactive Habit Optimization Runbook

A systematic, science-based approach to analyzing and optimizing your habits for better productivity, wellbeing, and goal achievement. Focuses on sustainable behavior change through environmental design, habit stacking, and incremental improvement.

## Purpose

This runbook helps you:
- **Analyze current habits** across all life areas with objective assessment
- **Identify habit opportunities** for productivity, health, relationships, and goals
- **Design sustainable habit changes** using proven behavior change principles
- **Create implementation systems** that support long-term habit maintenance
- **Integrate habit optimization** with your GTD workflow and life systems
- **Build habit awareness and monitoring** for ongoing optimization
- **Address habit obstacles** and create supportive environments

## Prerequisites

1. **Habit awareness** - Willingness to honestly assess current habits
2. **Change readiness** - Openness to experimenting with new behaviors
3. **GTD system** for integrating habit tracking and maintenance
4. **Time for gradual implementation** - Focus on sustainable change over quick fixes

## Instructions for AI Agent

This is a behavioral and systematic runbook. Ask **ONE question at a time**, focus on small sustainable changes over dramatic overhauls, and use evidence-based habit change principles. Be encouraging about incremental progress and realistic about change challenges.

**[Repeat design process for each priority habit change]**
