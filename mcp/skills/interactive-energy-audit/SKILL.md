# Interactive Energy Audit Runbook

A systematic, guided analysis of your energy patterns that reveals your natural rhythms, identifies energy optimization opportunities, and creates sustainable systems for maximizing your physical, mental, and emotional energy in alignment with your goals and values.

## Purpose

This runbook helps you:
- **Map your natural energy rhythms** across different time scales (daily, weekly, seasonal)
- **Identify energy drains and boosters** in your environment, activities, and relationships
- **Optimize task scheduling** based on energy levels rather than just time availability
- **Create sustainable energy management systems** that prevent burnout and maximize performance
- **Align energy allocation** with your most important goals and values
- **Build energy recovery and restoration practices** into your routine
- **Design lifestyle changes** that support optimal energy levels

## Prerequisites

1. **Several days of energy awareness** (or willingness to reflect on recent patterns)
2. **GTD system** with current tasks and projects for energy-task matching
3. **Honest self-assessment** mindset for analyzing patterns
4. **Commitment to experimentation** with energy optimization strategies

## Instructions for AI Agent

This is a reflective and physiological runbook. Ask **ONE question at a time**, help users recognize subtle energy patterns they might miss, and focus on sustainable changes rather than dramatic overhauls. Be encouraging about small improvements and natural rhythms.
