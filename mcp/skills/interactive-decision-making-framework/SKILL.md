# Interactive Decision Making Framework Runbook

A systematic, guided process for making major life and work decisions with confidence, clarity, and alignment with your values and long-term goals. Goes beyond simple pros/cons to provide comprehensive decision frameworks and implementation planning.

## Purpose

This runbook helps you:
- **Apply systematic decision-making frameworks** for complex choices
- **Clarify your values and priorities** as decision criteria
- **Analyze options thoroughly** with multiple perspectives and timeframes
- **Assess risks and opportunities** with scenario planning
- **Align decisions with long-term goals** and life vision
- **Create implementation plans** with decision integration into GTD system
- **Build decision confidence** through structured analysis and values alignment

## Prerequisites

1. **Specific decision** that needs to be made (career, relationship, financial, life direction)
2. **Decision timeline** - when the decision needs to be made
3. **GTD system** for integrating decision outcomes into projects and tasks
4. **Time for reflection** (45-90 minutes depending on decision complexity)

## Instructions for AI Agent

This is an analytical and values-based runbook. Ask **ONE question at a time**, help users explore options they might not have considered, and focus on both rational analysis and intuitive wisdom. Encourage thorough analysis while respecting decision timelines.

### Phase 1: Decision Framing & Context

**Step 1: Decision Definition**

```
🎯 **Welcome to Your Interactive Decision Making Framework!**

Let's work through your important decision with systematic clarity and confidence.

**Question 1:** Describe the decision you need to make and its current context.

**Decision Details:**
• **What specifically needs to be decided?** (Be as specific as possible)
• **When does this decision need to be made?** (Timeline/deadline)
• **What prompted this decision?** (What changed or what opportunity arose)
• **Who else is affected by this decision?** (Stakeholders, family, team, etc.)

**Current Situation:**
• **Status Quo:** What happens if you don't make a decision?
• **External Pressure:** Any time pressures or external forces?
• **Internal State:** How are you feeling about this decision? (excited, anxious, confused, etc.)

**Decision Importance:**
On a scale of 1-10, how much will this decision impact:
• Your next 1 year?
• Your next 5 years? 
• Your overall life satisfaction and goals?

[Understand decision scope, urgency, and importance]
```

### Phase 3: Option Generation & Analysis

**Step 5: Comprehensive Option Generation**

```
**Question 5:** Let's generate all possible options, including ones you might not have fully considered.

**Current Options You're Considering:**
List the options you've already identified:
1. [Option A]
2. [Option B]
3. [Option C]
[Continue with known options]

**Option Expansion Exercise:**
Let's think creatively about additional possibilities:

**"What if I had unlimited resources?"**
• What would you do if money, time, and skills weren't constraints?

**"What would I advise a friend in this situation?"**
• Sometimes we see options for others that we miss for ourselves.

**"What if I had to decide in 24 hours vs. 2 years?"**
• How do different timeframes change the options you'd consider?

**"What's the opposite of my obvious choice?"**
• If your inclination is A, what would the case for not-A look like?

**Creative Option Categories:**
• **Hybrid Options:** Combining elements of different choices?
• **Sequential Options:** Doing things in stages rather than all at once?
• **Third Alternatives:** Creative solutions that meet needs differently?
• **Status Quo Plus:** Improving current situation rather than changing it?

**Additional Options to Consider:**
Based on our exploration, what new options emerge?
4. [New option A]
5. [New option B]
[Continue with newly generated options]

[Generate comprehensive list of decision options]
```

**Step 6: Option Evaluation Matrix**

```
**Question 6:** Let's systematically evaluate each option against your decision criteria.

**Evaluation Matrix:**
For each option, rate how well it meets each criterion (1-10 scale):

**[Option 1: Name]**
• **[Value 1 Criterion]:** How well does this honor [value]? (1-10)
• **[Value 2 Criterion]:** Rating for [value]? (1-10)
• **[Continue for each criterion]**
• **Risk Level:** How risky is this option? (1-10, where 10 = very risky)
• **Implementation Difficulty:** How hard to execute? (1-10)
• **Reversibility:** How easy to change course later? (1-10)

**[Continue evaluation for each option]**

**Weighted Scoring:**
If some criteria are more important than others:
• Which criteria deserve 2x weight in your scoring?
• Any criteria that are 3x more important than others?

**Intuitive Check:**
Looking at the ratings:
• Any surprises in how options scored?
• Do the numbers align with your gut feelings?
• Any options that score well but feel wrong, or vice versa?

**Top Contenders:**
Based on systematic evaluation, which 2-3 options rise to the top?
1. [Highest scoring option]
2. [Second highest]
3. [Third or close alternative]

[Systematic evaluation of options against clear criteria]

### Phase 4: Scenario Analysis & Risk Assessment

**Step 7: Future Scenario Exploration**

```
**Question 7:** Let's explore how your top options might play out under different future scenarios.

**For Your Top Option: [Option Name]**

**Best Case Scenario (10% probability):**
• What's the most optimistic realistic outcome?
• What conditions would need to align for this best case?
• How would you feel and what would be possible?

**Most Likely Scenario (70% probability):**
• What's the realistic, probable outcome?
• What does success look like in normal circumstances?
• What challenges would you likely face and overcome?

**Challenging Scenario (15% probability):**
• What difficulties could reasonably occur?
• How would you adapt and respond to these challenges?
• What would you learn and how would you grow?

**Worst Case Scenario (5% probability):**
• What's the worst realistic outcome you should prepare for?
• How would you recover if things went poorly?
• What safety nets or backup plans make sense?

**Time Horizon Analysis:**
**6 Months:** How will this decision affect your life in 6 months?
**2 Years:** What will the impact be in 2 years?
**5 Years:** Looking back from 5 years, how might you view this decision?
**10 Years:** From a decade perspective, what will matter most?

**[Repeat scenario analysis for 2nd and 3rd options if helpful]**

[Explore realistic outcomes across different timeframes and scenarios]
```

**Step 8: Regret Minimization & Future Self Analysis**

```
**Question 8:** Let's use future perspective to minimize regret and ensure alignment with your long-term self.

**Regret Minimization Framework:**

**Future Self Consultation:**
Imagine yourself at age 80, looking back on your life:
• **For each top option:** How would 80-year-old you feel about this choice?
• **What would you regret NOT trying?**
• **What risks would you regret NOT taking?**
• **What would you regret spending/not spending time on?**

**Deathbed Test:**
This might sound dramatic, but it's clarifying:
• What decision would leave you with the least regret?
• What matters most when everything else falls away?
• Which path honors the life you want to have lived?

**Legacy Consideration:**
• How does each option contribute to the legacy you want to leave?
• What example do you want to set for others?
• How do you want to be remembered making decisions?

**Growth vs. Comfort Analysis:**
• Which option challenges you to grow the most?
• Which option feels most comfortable/safe?
• What's the right balance of growth vs. security for you right now?

**Future Flexibility:**
• Which option keeps the most doors open for future opportunities?
• Which option might limit future choices?
• What options does each choice create or eliminate?

**Regret Audit:**
For each top option, complete: "I would regret this choice if..."
- Option 1: I would regret this if...
- Option 2: I would regret this if...
- Option 3: I would regret this if...

[Apply long-term perspective and regret minimization analysis]
