# Interactive Values Clarification Runbook

A profound, systematic process for discovering, clarifying, and integrating your authentic core values into daily life, decision-making, and long-term vision. Goes beyond surface-level values exercises to create deep alignment between who you are and how you live.

## Purpose

This runbook helps you:
- **Discover your authentic core values** through deep self-exploration and life analysis
- **Distinguish true values from inherited or imposed beliefs** about what should matter
- **Assess current life alignment** with your clarified values across all major areas
- **Create practical value integration systems** for daily decision-making and life choices
- **Build value-based goal setting and planning** processes
- **Develop authentic confidence** from living in alignment with your deepest truths
- **Create legacy and contribution** that reflects your most important values

## Prerequisites

1. **Readiness for deep self-exploration** - Willingness to examine beliefs, assumptions, and life patterns
2. **Honest self-assessment** - Openness to seeing gaps between stated and lived values
3. **Dedicated reflection time** (2-3 hours or multiple sessions)
4. **Life experience to draw from** - Having lived enough to recognize patterns and preferences
5. **GTD system** for integrating values insights into goals and daily practices

## Instructions for AI Agent

This is a deeply reflective and potentially transformative runbook. Ask **ONE question at a time**, encourage honest self-examination without judgment, help users distinguish between authentic and inherited values, and focus on practical integration of insights. Be patient with contemplation time and support discovery of personal truth.
