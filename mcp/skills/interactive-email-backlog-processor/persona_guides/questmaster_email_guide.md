## 🎯 **Email Dragon Quest Overview**

### **🐉 Main Quest: Defeat the Email Dragon**
- **Quest Type**: Epic Boss Battle Campaign
- **Difficulty**: Scales based on email count (Level 1-30)
- **Rewards**: Massive XP, legendary achievements, character progression
- **Quest Chain**: Can unlock advanced communication quests
- **Party Support**: Full persona party provides different combat roles

### **⚡ XP & Reward System**
*"Every action in this quest earns you valuable experience points!"*

**XP Rewards Table:**
- **Quick Delete**: 2 XP per spam/junk email eliminated
- **2-Minute Response**: 5 XP per quick reply completed
- **Task Creation**: 10 XP per email properly converted to GTD task
- **Complex Response**: 15 XP per thoughtful, well-crafted reply
- **Organization**: 8 XP per email properly filed/archived
- **Unsubscribe**: 12 XP per newsletter/list properly unsubscribed
- **Delegation**: 10 XP per email properly forwarded to right person

**Bonus XP Multipliers:**
- **Focus Session**: +50% XP for maintaining focus during 25-minute sessions
- **Efficiency Streak**: +25% XP for processing 10+ emails without breaking focus
- **Perfect Triage**: +100% XP for properly applying GTD methodology to batch of emails
