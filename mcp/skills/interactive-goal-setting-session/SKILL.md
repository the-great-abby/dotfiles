# Interactive Goal Setting Session Runbook

A comprehensive, guided goal-setting experience that transforms vague aspirations into concrete GTD projects with clear milestones, accountability systems, and actionable next steps.

## Purpose

This runbook helps you:
- **Transform vague dreams** into specific, measurable goals
- **Apply SMART goal methodology** with interactive refinement
- **Break down goals** into GTD projects and actionable tasks
- **Create accountability systems** and progress tracking
- **Align goals with values** and life priorities
- **Set realistic timelines** with milestone celebrations
- **Integrate goals** seamlessly into your GTD workflow

## Prerequisites

1. **GTD system** properly configured for projects and tasks
2. **Dedicated time** for deep reflection (45-90 minutes)
3. **Access to calendar** for scheduling and timeline planning
4. **Quiet environment** for thoughtful consideration

## Instructions for AI Agent

This is a highly reflective and interactive runbook. Ask **ONE question at a time**, provide space for deep thinking, and help users discover their authentic goals. Use motivational language and celebrate insights.

### Phase 3: SMART Goal Formation

**Step 5: Goal Specification**

```
**Question 5:** Now let's turn your vision into a SMART goal.

**Your vision:** [Vision statement]

Let's make it Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, and Time-bound:

**SPECIFIC:** What exactly will you accomplish?
- Instead of "get healthier" → "run a 10K race"
- Instead of "earn more money" → "increase income by $20K annually"

**MEASURABLE:** How will you know you've succeeded?
- What numbers, metrics, or evidence will prove success?

**ACHIEVABLE:** Is this realistic given your resources and constraints?
- What makes this possible for you?
- What resources do you have or need?

**RELEVANT:** Why does this matter to you personally?
- How does this align with your values and priorities?

**TIME-BOUND:** When will you achieve this?
- Specific date or deadline?

Let's craft your SMART goal together!

[Iteratively refine until SMART criteria are met]
```

**Step 6: Goal Validation**

```
**Question 6:** Let's validate your goal to ensure it's truly yours.

**Your SMART Goal:** [Refined goal statement]

**Validation Questions:**

🎯 **Ownership Test:** Is this YOUR goal, or someone else's expectation?
⚡ **Energy Test:** On a scale of 1-10, how excited does this goal make you?
🌟 **Values Test:** How does this goal align with your core values?
🚀 **Stretch Test:** Will achieving this goal help you grow as a person?
⏰ **Timing Test:** Is this the right time in your life for this goal?
🛠️ **Capability Test:** Do you believe you can develop the skills to achieve this?

If any of these feel weak, let's adjust the goal until it feels authentically yours and energizing.

[Refine goal based on validation feedback]
```

### Phase 4: Obstacle Assessment & Strategy

**Step 7: Obstacle Identification**

```
**Question 7:** Let's honestly assess what could get in your way.

**Your Goal:** [Validated goal]

**Potential Obstacles:**
🚧 **Internal Obstacles:** What personal habits, fears, or patterns might interfere?
🌊 **External Obstacles:** What circumstances or other people might create challenges?
⏰ **Time Obstacles:** What time constraints or competing priorities exist?
💰 **Resource Obstacles:** What financial, skill, or tool limitations might arise?
🎭 **Motivation Obstacles:** When might you lose steam or enthusiasm?

For each obstacle you identify:
• How likely is it? (High/Medium/Low)
• How could you prevent or minimize it?
• If it happens, how would you handle it?

Honest obstacle assessment makes your goal plan much stronger!

[Identify and plan for major obstacles]
```

What combination feels most supportive and sustainable for you?

[Build comprehensive success strategy]
