# Interactive Annual Life Review Runbook

A comprehensive, reflective process for conducting meaningful annual reviews that celebrate the past year, extract key learnings, and create inspiring plans for the year ahead aligned with your evolving vision and values.

## Purpose

This runbook helps you:
- **Reflect deeply on the past year** with appreciation, learning, and integration
- **Celebrate achievements and growth** across all life areas
- **Process challenges and setbacks** with wisdom and perspective
- **Clarify evolving vision and values** for life direction
- **Create strategic plans for the year ahead** with inspiring goals and systems
- **Integrate insights** into your GTD system and daily practices
- **Build continuity and momentum** across years and life phases

## Prerequisites

1. **Dedicated time** for deep reflection (2-4 hours or multiple sessions)
2. **Quiet, comfortable environment** for contemplation and planning
3. **Past year's data** - calendars, journals, photos, achievements for review
4. **GTD system** for integrating insights and plans
5. **Openness to both celebration and honest self-assessment**

## Instructions for AI Agent

This is a reflective and visionary runbook. Ask **ONE question at a time**, encourage deep thinking and honest self-assessment, balance celebration with learning, and help users connect past insights to future vision. Be patient with contemplation time.
