# Interactive Someday/Maybe Review Runbook

A comprehensive, guided review of your someday/maybe list that transforms dormant ideas into actionable projects, cleans out stale items, and ensures your dreams stay alive and organized.

## Purpose

This runbook helps you:
- **Systematically review** all someday/maybe items
- **Activate ready ideas** into current projects
- **Clean out stale items** that no longer serve you  
- **Organize remaining dreams** by priority and timing
- **Prevent idea stagnation** through regular review cycles
- **Create activation triggers** for when timing becomes right
- **Maintain motivation** through dream connection and possibility thinking

## Prerequisites

1. **GTD system** with someday/maybe list or storage
2. **Dedicated time** for reflective review (30-60 minutes)
3. **Open mindset** for possibility and honest assessment
4. **Access to GTD tools** for project/task creation

## Instructions for AI Agent

This is a reflective and exploratory runbook. Ask **ONE question at a time**, encourage honest assessment, and help users stay connected to their dreams while being realistic about capacity and timing.

✅ Align with your current priorities and values

### Phase 4: Stale Item Processing

**Step 7: Stale Item Evaluation**

```
**Question 7:** Now let's honestly assess the items that feel stale or no longer compelling.

**Stale Items:**
[List items marked as cool/stale]

For items that no longer excite you, let's be honest about why:

**Common Reasons Items Go Stale:**
🕰️ **Life Phase Change** - Made sense then, doesn't fit now
🎯 **Priority Shift** - Other things became more important  
😴 **Fantasy vs Reality** - Sounded good but never felt actionable
👥 **Someone Else's Dream** - Was more about others' expectations
💸 **Resource Reality** - Never had/got the resources needed
🔄 **Outdated** - Circumstances changed, no longer relevant

For each stale item:
• What made you put this on someday/maybe originally?
• What's changed that makes it less appealing now?
• Any part worth preserving or transforming?

Be honest - it's okay to let go of dreams that no longer serve you!

[Process stale items with compassion and clarity]
```

**Step 8: Archive vs Delete Decisions**

```
**Question 8:** For the stale items, let's decide what to do with them.

**Options for Stale Items:**
🗑️ **Delete Completely** - No longer relevant, taking up mental space
📦 **Archive for Later** - Not now, but might resurface in different life phase
♻️ **Transform/Modify** - Core idea good, but needs different approach
🎁 **Gift to Others** - Good idea but better suited for someone else
📝 **Extract Learning** - Capture why it mattered then, what changed

**Item-by-Item Decisions:**
[Go through each stale item]

**[Stale Item 1]:** What feels right for this one?
- Delete, Archive, Transform, Gift, or Extract Learning?
- If archiving, under what future conditions might you revisit it?

[Process each stale item with appropriate action]
```
